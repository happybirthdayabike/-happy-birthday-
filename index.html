Abike Mi, Iyá Rere ❤️

Ẹ̀bùn Ìfẹ́ Ní Èdè Yorùbá

Abííkẹ́ mi, àwòrò nínú ilé, abiyamo tòótọ́ tí n mọ̀wùrù fún ọmọ rẹ̀. Ìyá mi, o jẹ́ nkan iyebíye tí kò ní iye, òdòdó tí kì í rẹ̀, àti ìmọ́lẹ̀ tó n tàn dídùn sí ọ̀nà mi nínú ayé yìí. Ẹnú kò le sọ tán, bẹ́ẹ̀ ni ọkàn kò le rò fún ohun gbogbo tí o ti ṣe fún mi. Ìfẹ́ rẹ dàbí odò tí kì í gbẹ, títọ́ rẹ sì tọ́ mi sọ́nà ire. Lónìí tí o n ṣe ayẹyẹ ọjọ́ ìbí rẹ, mo dúpẹ́ lọ́wọ́ Ọlọ́run fún ẹ̀mí rẹ. Ògo rẹ kò ní wọ̀mì, ayọ̀ rẹ kò ní dín kù, o kò ní fojú sunkún ọmọ mọ́. O tọ́ fún ìyìn, o tọ́ fún ayọ̀, o tọ́ láti jẹ́ olùfẹ́ pípẹ́ nínú ayé mi. Ìyá mi, mo nífẹ́ rẹ títí láé!

My Love Message in English

My dearest Abike, the one born to be coddled, loved, and treasured. You are the heartbeat of our home, a true and selfless mother who would give the world just to see her children smile. Mom, you are an irreplaceable gem, an unwithering flower, and the beautiful light that brightens up my path. Words can never fully capture, nor can my heart completely measure, the depth of my gratitude for everything you have sacrificed for me. Your love is an endless river, and your wisdom has safely guided my steps. As you celebrate your birthday today, I thank God for your life. May your glory never fade, may your joy never diminish, and may you never weep over your children. You deserve every ounce of celebration, praise, and endless honor. I love you beyond words, Mommy. Happy Birthday!
